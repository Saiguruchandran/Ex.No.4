# 1. Comparative Analysis Prompt
A Comparative Analysis Prompt is a prompting technique used to compare two or more concepts, technologies, products, or ideas in a structured manner. In this method, the AI identifies similarities, differences, advantages, disadvantages, features, and applications between the given topics. It helps users understand complex information clearly by presenting it side by side for easy analysis. Comparative prompting is widely used in education, research, business analysis, and decision-making processes because it allows users to evaluate multiple options effectively. The user usually provides the topics to compare along with specific comparison criteria such as features, performance, cost, applications, or benefits. The AI then analyzes the information and generates a detailed comparison in paragraph, bullet, or tabular format. For example, a user may ask the AI to compare Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning based on features, applications, and advantages. The AI explains that Artificial Intelligence is a broader field focused on creating intelligent systems, while Machine Learning is a subset of AI that enables systems to learn from data automatically. Comparative prompts improve clarity, analytical thinking, and understanding by organizing information systematically. They also help identify strengths and weaknesses of each topic efficiently. However, vague comparison criteria may lead to incomplete or general responses. To obtain better results, users should clearly specify the aspects to compare and use precise instructions. Overall, Comparative Analysis Prompting is an effective technique for structured evaluation, better understanding, and informed decision-making in various real-world applications.

# 2. Experiential Perspective Prompt
An Experiential Perspective Prompt is a prompting technique where the AI responds from a specific role, profession, or personal viewpoint to simulate real-life experiences and emotions. In this method, the user asks the AI to imagine itself as a particular person such as a doctor, teacher, engineer, student, or business leader and describe situations from that perspective. This technique helps create realistic, detailed, and emotionally connected responses that improve understanding and engagement. Experiential prompts are commonly used in storytelling, education, training, interviews, simulations, and creative writing because they provide practical insights into real-world experiences. For example, a user may ask, “Imagine you are a doctor during a pandemic. Describe your daily challenges and experiences.” The AI then explains the stress, responsibilities, emotional pressure, patient care, long working hours, and safety concerns faced during the pandemic. This prompting method improves empathy, creativity, communication, and contextual understanding. It also helps users explore different viewpoints and understand how people think or react in specific situations. However, unclear role descriptions may produce less detailed responses. To obtain better results, users should clearly mention the role, situation, tone, and perspective required. Overall, Experiential Perspective Prompting is an effective technique for generating human-like, immersive, and realistic AI responses based on practical experiences and viewpoints.

# 3. Everyday Functioning Prompts
Everyday Functioning Prompts are prompting techniques used for practical daily-life activities, routine planning, productivity support, and personal assistance. These prompts help users perform common tasks such as creating schedules, setting reminders, managing time, organizing activities, planning meals, tracking goals, and receiving recommendations. This technique is widely used because it makes AI function like a virtual assistant for everyday needs. For example, a user may ask, “Create a daily study timetable for a college student preparing for exams.” The AI then generates a structured schedule including study hours, breaks, revision time, and sleep routines. Everyday functioning prompts improve productivity, organization, efficiency, and time management in daily life. They are useful for students, professionals, families, and individuals who need assistance with routine planning and decision-making. These prompts are usually simple, direct, and highly practical. However, vague instructions may produce generic or less personalized outputs. To obtain accurate results, users should clearly specify goals, timing, preferences, and constraints. Overall, Everyday Functioning Prompts are highly effective for simplifying routine tasks, improving daily productivity, and helping users manage their activities efficiently using AI assistance.

# 4. Universal Prompt Structures / Prompt Refinements
Universal Prompt Structures, also called Prompt Refinements, are organized prompting methods that improve AI response quality by using clear instructions, context, constraints, and output formats. In this technique, the user carefully structures the prompt to guide the AI toward generating more accurate, detailed, and relevant responses. A refined prompt usually includes the role of the AI, the task to perform, specific requirements, tone, format, and limitations. For example, a user may ask, “Act as a software engineer. Explain cloud computing in simple language using bullet points and real-world examples.” This structured instruction helps the AI understand the expected response style and content clearly. Universal prompt structures are widely used in education, programming, business communication, research, content writing, and professional applications because they improve response precision and reduce ambiguity. They help users receive organized and high-quality outputs that match their exact needs. However, poorly structured prompts may lead to incomplete or irrelevant responses. To obtain better results, users should provide clear objectives, context, formatting instructions, and constraints. Overall, Universal Prompt Structures and Prompt Refinements are powerful techniques that enhance AI communication, improve response accuracy, and create more efficient and reliable interactions with AI systems.

Prompt engineering helps in designing clear instructions for AI models to generate useful responses. Different prompt patterns help AI provide better structured answers depending on the situation.

In this experiment, prompts are designed for a manufacturing automation system using IoT and embedded controllers. The responses generated by AI tools such as [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com?utm_source=chatgpt.com) and [Google Gemini](https://gemini.google.com?utm_source=chatgpt.com) are compared and evaluated using a rubric method.


# Procedure

# 1. Define the Scenario and Use Case

• Provide predictive maintenance alerts
• Improve energy efficiency in manufacturing processes

This helps industries reduce downtime and improve productivity.

Manufacturing industries such as:

• Automotive industry

These sectors can greatly benefit from automation and smart monitoring systems.

• Improve production efficiency by approximately 30%

# 2. Identify Prompt Patterns for Each Design Aspect

# Comparative Analysis Prompt

## Definition

Comparative prompts ask AI to compare two or more technologies or concepts and explain their differences and advantages.



## Prompt

“Compare IoT-based predictive maintenance with traditional manual maintenance in a manufacturing factory.”

## ChatGPT Output (Example)

IoT-based predictive maintenance uses sensors to monitor machine health continuously and predict failures before they occur. Traditional maintenance relies on periodic manual inspections and repairs after failures. Predictive maintenance reduces downtime and maintenance costs.


## Gemini Output (Example)

Predictive maintenance uses data from IoT sensors to detect early warning signs of equipment failure. Manual maintenance depends on scheduled checks or repairs after breakdown. Predictive systems improve efficiency and reduce operational disruptions.


# Experiential Perspective Prompt

## Definition

This prompt asks the AI to answer from the perspective of a particular role or professional experience.

## Prompt

“As a factory maintenance engineer, explain how IoT-based machine monitoring helps you manage equipment maintenance.”


## ChatGPT Output

IoT monitoring allows engineers to track machine health in real time, receive alerts when abnormal patterns occur, and schedule maintenance before breakdown happens.


## Gemini Output

From an engineer’s perspective, IoT sensors simplify maintenance planning by providing performance data and automated alerts, helping prevent unexpected machine failures.

# 3. Everyday Functioning Prompts

## Definition

Everyday functioning prompts explain technical ideas using simple real-life examples.



## Prompt

“Explain IoT predictive maintenance using an example similar to a health monitoring smartwatch.”


## ChatGPT Output

Just like a smartwatch tracks heart rate and activity to detect health issues, IoT sensors monitor machine temperature, vibration, and performance to detect possible failures.

## Gemini Output

IoT monitoring works like a smartwatch that tracks health conditions. If unusual patterns are detected, alerts are sent so action can be taken before problems occur.



# 4.Universal Prompt Structures / Prompt Refinements

## Definition

Universal prompt structures provide a clear template to explain processes step by step.



## Prompt

“Explain the working process of IoT-based predictive maintenance step by step.”



## ChatGPT Output

1. Sensors are installed on machines.
2. Sensors collect operational data.
3. Data is transmitted to monitoring systems.
4. AI analyzes patterns and detects anomalies.
5. Alerts are sent to engineers for maintenance.



## Gemini Output

The process begins with installing sensors on equipment. Data is collected and analyzed continuously. If abnormal conditions are detected, maintenance alerts are generated.


# 5. Prompt Size Limitations

## Definition

Large prompts are divided into smaller sections to make them easier for AI to process.

## Prompt

Large Prompt Example:
“Explain the entire IoT automation system used in a smart factory.”

## Improved Prompt (Chunking)

Phase 1: Describe the hardware components of an IoT system.
Phase 2: Explain how data is collected and transmitted.
Phase 3: Describe how predictive maintenance alerts are generated.



## ChatGPT Output

Breaking large prompts into smaller parts allows AI to produce clearer and more structured explanations.



## Gemini Output

Chunking large prompts improves response quality and helps organize complex information step by step.
